Why "Syntonal"?

The Syntonic Comma

In music theory, the syntonic comma is a minute, audible discrepancy (an 81:80 ratio) that represents a fundamental gap (21.51 cents) where the cyclic pathways of fifths and pure thirds fail to close into a perfect circle. It is the exact difference between a major third built by stacking perfect fifths, and a pure third derived from natural harmonics. Because these mathematical pathways do not naturally align, tuning forces an inescapable trade-off: perfect fifths create harsh thirds, while sweet thirds put fifths out of alignment.

The syntonic comma is a metaphor for the inevitable operational or systemic gaps that emerge within a complex organization or system when distinct, highly optimized pathways fail to seamlessly converge. When separate branches of an organization or system operate strictly by their own myopic internal logic — much like stacking perfect musical fifths — they inadvertently create a systemic fissure or friction that degrades the overall structural harmony and essential integration.

Solving for this conceptual "comma" requires insightful and creative compromise analogous to a tuning temperament, where the system intentionally considers the bigger picture and distributes holistic imperfection across multiple nodes rather than allowing a specific area to bear the entire burden where the loop can't be closed and the system collapses.

By implementing creative and clever solutions that wisely modify individual components, the gap is effectively closed, restoring the overarching internal alignment, systemic flexibility, and collaborative harmony necessary for the entire system to thrive.

Syntonal has the insights and experience to design, develop and implement big-picture problem-solving solutions that close gaps and restore organizational and systemic harmony.
